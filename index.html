Judicial primitive

The evidence is the law.

An automated verdict resolves most agreements. A dispute escalates to a jury that votes on evidence it can no longer see change.

01

Commitment

Two parties agree on an objective, acceptance criteria, and a GEN stake — escrowed before any work begins.

02

Evidence

Sources are submitted once and frozen into a snapshot — deduplicated, hashed, and never editable again.

03

Verdict

GenLayer reads the frozen evidence and returns FULFILLED, PARTIALLY_FULFILLED, BREACHED, or UNDETERMINED.

04

Appeal & jury

Either party can appeal. Staked jurors vote blind, then GenLayer checks their majority against the same evidence.

Docket actions

File & resolve.

Create agreement Tier 1

You become party_a. Your GEN stake is escrowed now; party_b must match it exactly to accept.

Default 1. Raise this for objectives that need independent corroboration rather than a single authoritative link. Duplicate URLs never count twice.

Accept / cancel

Only the named party_b can accept, by sending the exact same stake. Only party_a can cancel, and only before acceptance.

Submit evidence

Single-shot: the first successful call FREEZES the evidence set — no re-submission afterward. Either party may call this.

Resolve / finalize Tier 1

Permissionless. resolve_tier1 runs the automated verdict once evidence is locked. If nobody appeals in time, finalize_unappealed settles on it.

Become a juror Tier 2

Stake GEN independently of any single case. Selection weight is min(stake, cap) × reputation — a whale can't buy unlimited odds.

Appeal Tier 2

Either party, within the appeal window, posting a bond (~20% of stake, clamped). Selects a jury against the ALREADY-LOCKED evidence — needs at least 5 staked jurors.

Commit vote Tier 2

Only if you were selected. Your vote is hashed with a private salt before sending — nobody can see or copy it until you reveal.

Save your vote + salt somewhere — you need the exact same values to reveal.

Reveal vote Tier 2

Only after the commit window closes. Must match your earlier commit exactly, or it's rejected.

Finalize jury Tier 2

Permissionless, after the reveal window closes. Aggregates votes, checks the majority against frozen evidence, then applies slashing/rewards.

Withdraw

Sends your entire credited balance to your own wallet. The only method that ever moves GEN out of the contract.

Read the record.

Read-only calls — no wallet or GEN required.
